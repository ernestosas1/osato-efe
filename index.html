Our Story

Osato was just a girl focused on building her career in nursing, becoming independent and protecting the peace she had worked so hard to find. She had become so comfortable being on her own that she hardly let anyone get close. But deep down, she always hoped she would find her other half.

She even had a type: tall, athletic and white. 😂 Having grown up in Italy surrounded by Italians, she thought she knew exactly what her future would look like. But God clearly had other plans.

And then came Efe.

✨

How did you two meet?

It all started with our mums.

While my parents were in Nigeria for a year, my mum reconnected with Efe’s mum. As fate would have it, they discovered that both of their last born were single and decided that perhaps we should meet.

My mum encouraged me to give Efe a chance. I was absolutely not interested. 😂 But after a little persistence from my mum, I eventually agreed to one phone call just to make her happy. In my mind, it would be the first and last conversation we were going to have.

Well… I was very wrong.

That “one phone call” lasted nearly two hours and only ended because I actually had somewhere to be!

Somehow, that one conversation became the beginning of our love story. What started as our mothers trying to play matchmaker became something neither of us expected… our own little fairytale.

💫

Who made the first move? How did it happen?

What we do know is that our mums made the introduction, but the rest was entirely up to us. Somewhere between that first two-hour conversation, the endless conversations that followed and getting to know each other, the friendship and connection began to grow.

So perhaps the real answer is: our mums made the first move, and we took it from there. ❤️

💪

What’s your favorite activity to do together?

Going to the gym! I am passionate about fitness and love being active, so naturally Efe has had to promise me that one day he’ll get those six-pack abs. 😂

Whether we’re actually working out or just talking, laughing and challenging each other, the gym has become one of the things we enjoy doing together.

✈

Where are you going for your honeymoon?

Anywhere with gorgeous beaches!

🍉

What’s the best meal you’ve shared together?

Pepper soup. Of course! 😂❤️

Although there was one particular bowl of pepper soup that Efe made so spicy that I genuinely questioned whether this was how our love story was going to end, as Efe was attempting for my life!

Thankfully, I survived.

And despite Efe nearly ending my life with that very spicy bowl, pepper soup remains one of our favourite meals and a very memorable part of our story!

Apparently, true love is surviving Nigerian pepper soup together. 😂❤️
